A line like 1 Sol Ring (CMM) 410 names its exact printing, so it is used as written, for Archive and Vault alike. The source you choose below is the secondary source: it only decides the cards whose line does not state a set. (AI art ignores this: Forge draws everything.)

Style

A LoRA trained on photos drags the picture toward a photograph. With this on, those pictures get cartoon style words and then a second pass through the plain cartoon model. Higher keeps more of the cartoon look but loosens the likeness; lower keeps the likeness but looks more like a photo. Costs a few extra seconds per card.
